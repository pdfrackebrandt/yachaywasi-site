Yachaywasi

yachay wasi, Quechua for house of knowledge

Papers get checked before they get judged. Yachaywasi reruns the authors' code, checks the maths, and lets AI models read a blinded copy and say what they think.

Three checks, one report

  1. Code

    The numbers come out again

    Give it a paper and its replication package. It reruns the code in a sealed sandbox and compares every number it regenerates with the numbers the authors shipped, then looks for those numbers in the paper itself.

  2. Maths

    The claims are stated and tried

    It finds the propositions and theorems, writes each one for a computer algebra system and, where it can, for a machine checked proof in Lean. A claim it cannot state is marked as such. Nothing is guessed.

  3. Opinion

    Referees without the names

    Free AI models read a copy with authors, affiliations and links removed, and score importance, novelty and method. Their answers are shown as opinion. They are never shown as a pass, a fail or a forecast.

Accuracy is claimed for the code rerun and the maths check, and for nothing else.

One paper, start to finish

Measuring Markets for Network Goods, with its public R replication package.

Code
Reproduced. All four modules ran in 90 seconds. 492 regenerated numbers matched the authors' outputs, with no mismatches.
Maths
No verdict. The paper has one formal proposition, and the small model used could not state it, so nothing was proved either way.
Opinion
Two open models gave their reading. Shown as opinion only; this paper is already published, so it is no test of them.

"Reproduced" means the package regenerates its own outputs exactly and those numbers appear in the paper. It says nothing about whether the ideas are right. People judge the ideas.

Can a blind read tell good work from good names?

Behind the tool sits a research question. If a model reads thousands of economics working papers with every name and affiliation removed, can it tell which ones will reach a top journal, and does it see anything the authors' reputations do not already show?

A paper on that is in progress. Its results will appear here once it is out.

Where it stands
